IMAGE MANAGEMENT
================

How to Update Images:

1. Replace Banner Background:
   - Replace file: img/banner-background.jpg
   - CSS automatically references this file
   - Used in hero section background

2. Update Product Images:
   - Replace product-1.jpg through product-6.jpg
   - Same naming convention required
   - Located in img/ folder

3. Update Service Icons:
   - Replace service-1.png through service-4.png
   - Keep PNG format for transparency

4. Update Testimonial Photos:
   - Replace testimonial-1.jpg through testimonial-3.jpg
   - Square format recommended for carousel

Image Naming Convention:
  - Use lowercase with hyphens: banner-background.jpg
  - Avoid underscores or misspellings
  - Keep consistent naming for easy management
